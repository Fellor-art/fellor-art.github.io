About Me

Hello! I'm Fellor, a Hungarian pixel artist and animator with nearly three years of experience working in Aseprite.

I specialize in high-quality pixel art visual effects, animation and game assets. My VFX packs are designed to integrate smoothly into Unity projects and include organized files, prefabs and clear documentation.

I am also one of the co-owners of BitBlast Studio, where I work on visual design, animation, effects and other graphical elements for our game projects.
